A short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act. Two agents,
two keys, one door: neither key opens anything on its own, and the key kept
safe in a pocket opens less the longer it stays there. Turning together is
the agreement, the small machine that joins two small strengths into one
act, and the room neither of them could enter alone.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt` (203 characters). No dependencies,
no network.
